RobustGait: Robustness Analysis for Appearance-Based Gait Recognition

Reeshoon Sayera1, A. Kumar1, S. Mitra1, P. Kamtam1
1 University of Central Florida
WACV 2026
📄 Paper 📝 arXiv 💻 Code 📑 Slides 🖼️ Poster

Abstract

Appearance-based gait recognition has shown impressive performance on controlled datasets. However, robustness against real-world corruptions and silhouette errors remains significantly underexplored. We introduce RobustGait, a comprehensive robustness benchmark evaluating six popular gait recognition architectures under 15 corruption types across five severity levels. Additionally, we analyze the impact of human-parsing and segmentation quality on downstream gait recognition. Our results reveal that models are highly susceptible to digital and temporal corruptions, with performance drops up to 65% under severe noise.

Code and project page: https://github.com/Reeshoon/RobustGait

Method Overview

RobustGait consists of (1) high-quality silhouette parsing, (2) controlled corruption simulation, and (3) evaluation of state-of-the-art gait recognition models across multiple datasets.

Sample Results

Rank-1 accuracy decreases consistently as noise severity increases across CASIA-B, CCPG, and SUSTech1K. Silhouette IoU strongly correlates with downstream recognition performance.
